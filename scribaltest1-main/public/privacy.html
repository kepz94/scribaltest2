1. Information we handle

Scribal is built so that your study stays with you. The information involved falls into a few groups:

  • Study content you create. The marks you make (highlights, underlines, bold, circles), the themes and labels you assign, any notes and compilations you build, and the chapter you last read. By default, this content is stored only on your own device.
  • Account information (only if you sign in). If you choose to sign in with Google to sync across devices, Scribal receives your Google account’s email address and basic profile information. This is used to identify your account and associate your synced study content with you.
  • Synced study content (only if you enable sync). If you turn on syncing, the study content described above is stored in the cloud so it can appear on your other devices. Synced content is stored [DESCRIBE WHERE SYNCED DATA IS STORED — e.g. “in Google Drive application data tied to your account” or “on our servers”].
  • A device identifier. Scribal generates a random identifier stored on your device to help manage syncing between your devices. It is not tied to your real-world identity.

2. How we use information

Scribal uses the information above only to:

  • provide the app and keep your study content available to you;
  • sign you in and sync your study content across your devices, if you choose to enable that; and
  • maintain, secure, and improve the app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your study content or account information to show you advertising.
